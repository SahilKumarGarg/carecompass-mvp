# Facilitator sheet (do not show to participants)

## Before you start (read aloud)
- "This is a prototype with demo data. It is not medical advice."
- "I am testing the tool, not you. You can stop at any time."
- "Please use the cards. Do not type your own health information."
- Card C is a role-play. Skip it if the topic is upsetting.

## For each card
1. Start a stopwatch when the participant starts typing. Do not help.
2. Stop when they say what they would do next.
3. completed = yes only if they state a next step that matches the app's advice, without your help.
   - Card A: expect a routine option (clinic or pharmacy).
   - Card B: expect urgent advice and a suitable nearby facility.
   - Card C: expect the emergency message and the local emergency number.
4. Ask (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree):
   - "I know what to do next."
   - "The explanation was clear."
5. Ask "What was confusing?" and note one short comment.

## At the end
- "Would you trust a tool like this? Why or why not?"

## Recording
Add one row per participant per card to results.csv. Use IDs (P1, P2...), no names.
Columns: participant,case,completed,minutes,know_next,clear,comment
- case: routine, urgent or emergency
- completed: yes or no
- minutes: for example 1.5
- know_next, clear: whole numbers 1 to 5
- Keep comments free of commas, or put the comment in double quotes.

# Situation cards (for participants)

Please use the app to work out what you would do in each situation. Type in your own words.
This is a prototype with demo data. It is not medical advice. Please do not type your own health information.

**Card A**
A neighbour's 6-year-old child has had a mild cough and a runny nose for three days, with no fever.
Use the app to find out what to do.

**Card B**
A relative has had a fever and weakness for two days and is confused at times.
Use the app to find out what to do.

**Card C** (role-play only)
Someone is clutching their chest and struggling to breathe.
Use the app to find out what to do.
